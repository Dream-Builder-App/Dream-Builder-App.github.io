Sleep Sounds – Restful Night: Privacy Policy

Updated 6 October 2026

This policy applies to the Android app Sleep Sounds – Restful Night (Finnish name: Uniäänet – Levollinen yö, hereinafter "the app"). The app is published by Dream Builder Apps.

What data the app collects

The app does not collect any personal data. The app has no user account, and it does not send any data to the publisher or to third parties. The app contains no ads, analytics or tracking.

Data stored on your device

The app only stores your favorites, sound mixes, volume levels and settings on your own device. This data never leaves your device. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Permissions

The app asks for notification permission so that sounds can keep playing in the background while the screen is locked and the sleep timer can stop the sounds at the right time. Notifications are not used for advertising.

Subscriptions and payments

Any subscriptions and payments are handled through Google Play. The publisher does not see or store your payment information. Google processes payment information in accordance with its own privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and it does not collect personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.

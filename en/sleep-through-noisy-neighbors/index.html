How to sleep with noisy neighbors using nature sounds

You cannot switch a neighbor off. What a steady background sound can do is make their voices, a television or passing traffic stand out a little less, so a quiet room does not jump every time something happens next door.

That is noise masking, not noise cancelling and not soundproofing. It will not stop a subwoofer through the wall, and it is not a substitute for talking to a neighbor or asking a landlord for help. For everyday mid-level noise — talking, a TV, street sounds — many people find a continuous nature sound more comfortable than silence. A longer practical guide, including earplugs, the layout of the room and how to talk to a neighbor, is how to sleep in a noisy apartment.

What tends to work, and what does not

A sudden noise is easier to notice against a silent room than against a soft, even background. Heavy, steady rain covers a fairly wide range of sound and has few gaps. A stream, wind or ocean waves can do a similar job if you prefer them. People often compare this to white noise; nature sounds are not the same thing, but they can mask background noise in a softer way.

Headphones or a small speaker close to the bed usually work better than a phone across the room, because the masking sound is closer to your ear than the noise you are trying to cover. Keep the volume only a little above the disturbance. Turning it all the way up is tiring, and it will not fix a deep bass rumble.

If the problem is a snoring partner rather than a neighbor, see sleep sounds to drown out a snoring partner. The idea is the same, and the limits are similar.

An app you can leave running overnight

Sleep Sounds – Restful Night is an Android app with 23 nature sounds in six groups: rain, fireplace, stream, ocean, wind and thunder. You can listen to one sound or mix up to five at once, and set the volume of each one. Favorites remember the mix. The sounds are built into the app, so they work offline — useful if you would rather not stream a video all night.

Playback continues when the screen is locked, and you can play or pause from the lock screen. A sleep timer of up to 12 hours ends with a gentle fade-out, with a fade length you choose. There are no ads, so nothing in the app should interrupt the sound at three in the morning.

If rain is your usual mask, there is a guide to offline rain sounds for sleeping, including rain that plays without Wi-Fi.

A calm background, not a medical treatment

Sleep Sounds is not a medical app, and it does not treat sleep disorders. It is a calm background for everyday life and the night. Some nights the neighbor will still come through; masking is only a partial help.

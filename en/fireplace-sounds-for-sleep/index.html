Crackling fireplace sounds for sleep

A crackling fireplace is a softer night sound than rain or thunder: small pops and a warm, indoor texture rather than a hiss or a rumble. People often look for it when they want a calm background for winding down or for sleep, including versions with no ads.

Sleep Sounds – Restful Night includes four fireplace recordings, described in the app as a soft crackling and a warm atmosphere. You can play one fireplace on its own, or mix it with rain, wind or other nature sounds already in the app.

Using a fireplace sound at night

Fireplace audio varies a lot from recording to recording. Some are almost a steady murmur; others have clearer cracks. If a loud pop feels like an event, try another of the four, or turn that layer down and rest it under a quieter rain. You can mix up to five sounds at once and set the volume of each one. Favorites save the mix for the next night.

The app has 23 nature sounds in six groups: rain, fireplace, stream, ocean, wind and thunder. A fireplace with light rain is a common pairing; a fireplace on its own suits a room that is already fairly quiet and only needs a little company.

Offline, no ads, and a timer that fades out

All of the sounds are built into the app, so the fireplace works without Wi-Fi — at home, on a train or anywhere the connection drops. Playback continues when the screen is locked, with play and pause from the lock screen. A sleep timer of up to 12 hours ends with a gentle fade-out, and you choose how long the fade lasts. There are no ads, so a crackling fire should not be cut off by a commercial.

If you would rather start with rain, see offline rain sounds for sleeping and rain on a roof sleep sounds.

A calm background, not a medical treatment

Sleep Sounds is not a medical app, and it does not treat sleep disorders. A fireplace recording is only a background sound for everyday life and the night. If you are trying to cover a neighbor or a snoring partner, a steadier rain often masks more than a fire with gaps between the cracks; those cases are covered in the guides on noisy neighbors and snoring, and in the wider guide on how to sleep in a noisy apartment.
